Services, sheet by sheet

Beam calculations & wall removals

Extensions & loft conversions

The consultancy

Birmingham built,
client led

MB Design Solutions is a Birmingham-based structural consultancy led by Mohammed Bilal, working from Fort Dunlop on projects of all types and sizes across the West Midlands and beyond.

With over fifteen years' experience designing to British Standards and Eurocodes, the practice has delivered everything from single-beam wall removals to residential developments, structural surveys, temporary works and commercial, industrial and healthcare projects.

The approach is simple and client-led: one point of contact, efficient designs, honest advice, and calculations that building control accepts — everything your project needs from a single place.
